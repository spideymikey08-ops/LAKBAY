Travel recommendations

Smart tips for a seamless Baguio mountain holiday

Tip 1

Pack Warm Layers

Temperatures can drop sharply in the late afternoon and evenings (12°C–16°C). Always bring a windbreaker, sweater, and comfortable walking shoes.

Tip 2

Start Early to Beat Traffic

Baguio's popular routes around Burnham, Wright Park, and Mines View get busy after 10 AM. Visit nature spots early morning for serene pine views.

Tip 3

Taste Local Specialties

Don't miss hot strawberry taho from local vendors, freshly baked raisin bread from Baguio Country Club, and Good Shepherd Convent ube jam.
