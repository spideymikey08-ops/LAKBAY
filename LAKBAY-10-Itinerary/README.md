## 🌲 Project Overview

**Lakbay** is an interactive, event-driven Single Page Application (SPA) designed to help travelers discover tourist attractions across **Baguio City** (The Summer Capital of the Philippines), organize step-by-step travel schedules, and estimate travel expenses with real-time budget calculations.

All user data (itinerary stops and budget estimates) seamlessly persists in the browser via `localStorage`, with defensive isolated `try/catch` wrappers and schema shape validation to ensure plans survive page refreshes and never crash the app.

```

├── index.html               ← GENERATED by build.php (open this in the browser)

│   ├── explore.css
│   └── explore.js
├── itinerary/               ← Screen 4 · Itinerary & budget module
│   ├── itinerary.html
│   ├── itinerary.css
│   └── itinerary.js
├── summary/                 ← Screen 7 · Summary module
│   ├── summary.html
│   ├── summary.css
│   └── summary.js
├── partials/                ← shared HTML: sidebar, modals, popover, toast
├── js/                      ← shared JavaScript: data, core (incl. appearance.js), components, app.js
├── css/style.css            ← shared base styles + global responsive rules
├── css/editorial.css        ← magazine-style layout, hero, gallery, guide, final CTA
├── css/palette.css          ← swappable nature-editorial color theme
├── css/night-mode.css       ← night theme applied via the sidebar appearance toggle
└── assets/images/
```

**Workflow**: edit a module file in `home/`, `explore/`, `itinerary/`, or `summary/`,
then run `php build.php` (XAMPP: `C:\xampp\php\php.exe build.php`) to regenerate
`index.html`. JS changes do not need a rebuild unless you add or rename a file;
in that case update the `<script>` list in `index.template.html` and rebuild.

The scripts are plain (non-module) scripts that share one global scope, so the app
still works when `index.html` is opened directly from disk. **Script order in
`index.template.html` matters**: shared JS → module JS → `app.js` (last).

## 📱 Navigation & Application Screens

### 0. Left-Side Navigation Bar (ANI-12 Style Layout)
- Fixed forest-green sidebar (`#103B2D`) with a lime active pill (`#D6EF4A`), cream labels, SVG icons, a day/night appearance toggle, and a bottom "Plan Trip" shortcut pill.
- Smoothly transforms into a horizontal touch-scrolling header on mobile devices (tested down to 360px).

### 1. Screen 1 · Home Editorial Landing (`#section-home`)
- **Hero**: Asymmetrical split layout with "FIND YOUR WAY TO BAGUIO.", supporting copy, primary/secondary CTAs, and a large rounded mountain photograph.
- **Introduction**: "A CITY ABOVE THE ORDINARY." with editorial copy, four travel statistic cards, and the live trip-progress card.
- **Featured Destinations**: Magazine-style gallery with quick-add to itinerary and carousel pagination dots.
- **Experience Baguio**: Six photographic cards covering food, cafes, strawberry picking, markets, culture, and nature.
- **Travel Guide**: Six compact cards for transport, costs, dining, tips, packing, and visitor reminders.
- **Final CTA**: Full-width scenic closing section with "CREATE MY ITINERARY".

### 2. Screen 2 · Explore Gallery (`#section-explore`)
- **Live Search**: Instant keyword filtering across destination names, areas, descriptions, and activities.
- **Category Chips**: Filter by `All`, `Nature & Parks`, `Culture & Heritage`, `Food & Shopping`, `Viewpoints`, and `Museums & Arts`.
- **Fee Filter Chips**: Filter by `All`, `Free Only`, `Under ₱50`, `Under ₱100`.
- **Rating Filter**: Multi-attribute dropdown for `Any Rating`, `★ 4.7 & up`, and `★ 4.8 & up`.
- **Sort Dropdown**: Sort by Name (A-Z, Z-A), Rating (High to Low), or Entrance Fee (Low to High, High to Low).
- **Interactive Baguio Map View**: Toggleable Leaflet/OpenStreetMap embed displaying all 20 Baguio destinations with custom clickable popups.
- **SessionStorage Persistence**: Active filter preferences are saved in `sessionStorage` so refreshing or switching screens preserves your search.

### 3. Screen 3 · Destination Details Modal (`#destination-modal`)
- Accessible modal dialog with focus trap and focus restoration on close.
- Displays high-resolution photography, category, entrance fee, rating, and location.
- **New Detailed Metadata**:
  - 📍 **Area Cluster**: Downtown / Burnham, Mines View Ridge, Session Road, Camp John Hay, etc.
  - 🕒 **Operating Hours**: Exact daily schedules.
  - 🌅 **Best Time to Visit**: Advice on morning mist, avoiding family crowds, and sunset viewpoints.
  - 🚌 **How to Get There**: Practical jeepney and walking directions.
  - 💡 **Local Baguio Tip**: Insider advice (e.g. rent paddleboats early, try hot strawberry taho).
  - 🗺️ **Open in Google Maps**: Direct external navigation link.
- Action buttons: "Add to itinerary", Close (`&times;`), and `Escape` key shortcut.

### 4. Screen 4 · Travel Itinerary Planner with Integrated Budget Planning (`#section-itinerary`)
- **Trip Setup**: Always-visible controls for travel date, number of travelers, and trip duration. Changes persist and re-align scheduled stops.
- **Step 1: Budget Range Selection Before Planning (View A)**:
  - Interactive dual-range slider for minimum and maximum travel budget in Philippine Pesos (₱500 – ₱25,000).
  - Quick preset chips: *Backpacker* (₱1,500 – ₱3,500), *Standard* (₱3,500 – ₱8,000), and *Comfort* (₱8,000 – ₱18,000).
  - Two-way bound numerical input fields and active highlight slider track.
  - Live per-day rate computation (`Est. ₱X – ₱Y per day for N days`).
  - Confirmation button: *"Confirm Budget & Start Planning Itinerary &rarr;"*.
- **Step 2: Travaa-Style Timeline Planner (View B - Strictly No Map)**:
  - **Trip Overview Strip**:
    - Compact summary items for travel dates, travelers, trip duration, and total budget.
  - **Reference Timeline Layout**:
    - Each day header keeps the full date (e.g. `Day 1 · Nov 25, 2026`) and a pale-sage stat bar; the 7 AM–12 midnight hour labels sit as a faint background layer inside the day's timeline grid, directly below the header. The full evening scale stays available and stops can be dragged into late slots up to midnight.
    - Lime day-filter pills (`All Days (2)`, `Day 1`, `Day 2`) and a circular dark-green add button.
    - Pale-sage day bars with stop count, total duration, entrance-fee total, and a per-day `+ Add to Day` action.
    - Green time-positioned stop blocks; the first stop of each day uses its destination photo as the card background, with travel-time hop pills between stops and a green end arrow.
    - Custom bottom scrollbar with left/right controls for the horizontally scrollable timeline.
    - Empty day prompt: Inviting empty slot banner when a day has 0 stops.
    - **Schedule Clash & Overlap Detection**: Detects overlapping stops on the same day, highlights clashing blocks with an amber warning outline and `⚠️` badge, and displays an explicit clash warning in the Stop Inspector.
  - **Right Stop Inspector Panel**:
    - Simplified to three fields only: **Planned Activity** dropdown, **Where do you want to go?** autocomplete, and **Trip Day**.
    - The Planned Activity dropdown is populated from the selected destination's activities.
    - Existing timing, budget, and contact data are preserved internally without extra form fields.
    - Actions: `Add to Itinerary` / `Save Changes`, `Remove` (when editing), and `Cancel`.
  - **Sample Plan & Export**:
    - Sample plan button populates a 2-day budget-conscious Baguio itinerary.
    - Copy Plan outputs formatted text with full budget breakdown, target range, remaining funds, and day-by-day stops.

### 6. Screen 6 · Travel Budget Calculator (`#section-budget`)
- **Automatic Activities Fee Sync**: Entrance fees are computed automatically as `sum(stop.fee) * travelers` after add, edit, delete, undo, clear, or travelers count change.
- **Other Activities & Shopping Field**: Separate input for pasalubong, strawberry treats, and Cordillera weaves so stop entrance fees stay accurate.
- **Accommodation Nights Calculation**: Accurately computes nights as `Math.max(1, days - 1)` with live rate hint: `₱X.XX / night (N night(s) for D day(s))`.
- **Budget Presets**:
  - 🎒 *Backpacker*: ₱1,200/day/traveler (jeepneys, transient rooms, street eats)
  - 🌲 *Standard*: ₱3,200/day/traveler (taxis, 3-star boutique hotel, cafes)
  - ✨ *Comfort*: ₱6,500/day/traveler (private van, Camp John Hay Manor, specialty dining)
- **Visual Comparison Progress Bar**: Live progress bar comparing scheduled stop entrance fees against your allocated activities budget with status badge ("Within Budget" / "Exceeds Budget").

### 7. Screen 7 · Travel Plan Summary (`#section-summary`)
- Compiles trip duration, traveler counts, day-by-day itinerary schedule (with per-day fee subtotals and completed badges), and itemized budget table into a print-ready report.
- Actions: "Copy Plan" (clipboard), "Export JSON" (backup), and "Print / Save PDF" (clean print layout omitting buttons and navigation).

## ⚡ Event Map Implementation (Events E1 to E20+)

Every interaction strictly follows Event-Driven Programming standards:

| Event ID | Feature | User Action | DOM Event | JavaScript Handler | Expected Result |
| :---: | :--- | :--- | :---: | :--- | :--- |
| **E1** | App Bootstrap | Load or refresh page | `DOMContentLoaded` | `init()` | Restores `localStorage` and `sessionStorage`, configures date constraints, and renders views. |
| **E2** | Navigation | Click sidebar link or CTA | `click` | `showSection(name)` | Switches active section, updates floating active white pill, updates browser URL hash, closes mobile menu. |
| **E3** | Explore Search | Type keyword in search bar | `input` | `searchInput.addEventListener` | Live filters 21 destinations by name, area, location, description, or activity; updates counter. |
| **E4** | Explore Category | Click category chip | `click` | `categoryChips.addEventListener` | Filters cards by category; highlights active chip; persists preference in `sessionStorage`. |
| **E5** | Explore Price | Click price filter chip | `click` | `priceChips.addEventListener` | Filters by Free, <₱50, <₱100; updates cards gallery and counter. |
| **E6** | Explore Rating | Change rating dropdown | `change` | `filterRating.addEventListener` | Filters for destinations rated ★ 4.7+ or ★ 4.8+. |
| **E8** | Explore Sorting | Change sort dropdown | `change` | `sortDropdown.addEventListener` | Reorders cards alphabetically, by rating, or by entrance fee. |
| **E9** | Interactive Map | Click "Show Map" button | `click` | `mapToggleBtn.addEventListener` | Expands Leaflet map container and plots 20 Baguio GPS pins with custom interactive popups. |
| **E10** | Destination Details | Click "Details" button | `click` | `showDetails(id)` | Opens accessible modal with hours, best time, directions, local tip, and Google Maps link; traps keyboard focus. |
| **E11** | Destination Details | Click Close or press `Esc` | `click`, `keydown` | `closeDetails()` | Closes modal and returns focus to the triggering button. |
| **E13** | Quick-Add Itinerary | Click "+ Itinerary" on card | `click` | `openQuickAddPopover(id)` | Opens lightweight popover to schedule stop with date, time, duration without leaving Explore. |
| **E14** | Itinerary Scheduling | Submit itinerary form | `submit` | `saveItineraryStop()` | Validates inputs, detects time overlaps and distant hops with soft warnings, sorts stops, and auto-syncs budget. |
| **E15** | Itinerary Edit | Click "Edit" on stop card | `click` | `editStop(id)` | Pre-populates form with stop details, duration, time, and switches button to "Update stop". |
| **E16** | Itinerary Delete | Click "Delete" on stop | `click` | `deleteStop(id)` | Removes stop, auto-recalculates budget, and displays toast with a 4.5-second "Undo" button. |
| **E17** | Itinerary Clear All | Click "Clear all" button | `click` | `clearBtn.addEventListener` | Displays custom confirmation dialog; clearing removes all stops and resets budget activities fee. |
| **E18** | Sample Plan Loader | Click "Start with Sample Plan" | `click` | `loadSample1DayPlan()` | Populates 5 iconic Baguio stops on today's date, synchronizes budget fees, and updates progress to 100%. |
| **E19** | Budget Calculator | Input expense or traveler | `input` | `calculateBudget()` | Live recomputes Total, Per-traveler, and accommodation per night (`days - 1`); updates comparison bar. |
| **E20** | Budget Presets | Click Backpacker / Standard / Comfort | `click` | `applyBudgetTier(tier)` | Auto-fills recommended daily rates for transport, lodging, food, and shopping based on days and travelers. |
| **E21** | Plan Export & Share | Click "Copy Plan" / "Export JSON" | `click` | `copyPlanAsText()`, `exportJSON()` | Copies formatted itinerary to clipboard or downloads `.json` file backup. |
| **E22** | Plan Import | Select JSON file | `change` | `importJSON(file)` | Reads file via `FileReader`, validates schema, restores state, and refreshes UI. |
