The Beauty of the Highlands

A City Above the Ordinary.

Baguio rests high in the Cordillera mountains, where cool pine-scented air meets a culture shaped by Igorot heritage. Spend a morning beside Burnham Park's lagoon, follow the scent of brewed coffee down Session Road, taste strawberry taho from a neighborhood vendor, then climb toward a ridge where the clouds settle over the city. Every turn reveals another reason to linger a little longer.

From heritage gardens and museum galleries to strawberry farms and night markets, this is a place best explored slowly — and planned well.

Travel Guide

The practical details, beautifully simple.

Everything you need before you go, gathered in one quiet place.

Getting there

By bus: roughly 4–6 hours from Manila to Baguio. Around town: jeepneys, taxis, and ride-hailing services cover most destinations.

Estimated expenses

Budget around ₱1,500–₱3,500 for a modest two-day trip, excluding intercity transport. Entrance fees are usually ₱0–₱200.

Where to eat

Start with strawberry taho and raisin bread, then try Cordilleran specialties, hot chocolate, and a slow dinner along Session Road.

Travel tips

Visit popular spots before 10 AM, keep small bills handy, and check opening days for museums before finalizing your route.

Packing essentials

Bring a light jacket, comfortable walking shoes, an umbrella, a reusable bottle, and a small pouch for cash and IDs.

Good to remember

Respect heritage sites, follow local jeepney routes, and leave extra time for the winding roads into the mountains.
