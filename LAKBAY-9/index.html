Experience Baguio

The city is best tasted slowly.

Follow the small rituals that make a highland visit feel like your own.

01 / FLAVORS

Local food & restaurants

Warm soups, raisin bread, and Cordilleran dishes served in homegrown kitchens.

02 / SLOW MOMENTS

Cafes & coffee shops

Benguet coffee, thick hot chocolate, and a window seat beneath the pines.

03 / THE FIELDS

Strawberry picking

Head to the outskirts for cool air, sweet berries, and a basket worth bringing home.

04 / AFTER DARK

Shopping & local markets

From ukay finds on Harrison Road to handcrafted souvenirs and jars of ube jam.

05 / HERITAGE

Cultural attractions

Ancient stonework, living traditions, and galleries shaped by Cordilleran artists.

06 / OUTDOORS

Nature & open air

Pine trails, garden paths, and ridge views made for unhurried mornings.
